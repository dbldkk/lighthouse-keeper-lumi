# The Lighthouse Keeper

**An isometric survival horror on a northern island. 14 days, 14 nights, one lighthouse.**

You've been dropped on the island to relieve the previous keeper. By day you fish, chop wood, fix
the generator and haul oil up to the lamp. By night you keep the light burning. As long as the beam
sweeps the water, nothing touches you. When it goes dark, **the Hunter** comes out of the treeline:
first just a glint of eyes, then footsteps by the cabin.

### Features
- **Light is life.** Lantern, lighthouse, cabin lamp. Darkness eats your sanity, then your strength, and the game tells you clearly when it's happening.
- **The Hunter** tracks you by light and noise, breaks down doors, and doesn't forgive mistakes. Hide in wardrobes, under tarps or behind barrels.
- **Special nights:** a storm cuts the power, fog swallows everything past arm's length, a fishing boat
  drifts toward the rocks and you have to guide it with the signal lamp, and a "rescuer" knocks on your door…
  but there are no boat lights at the pier.
- **Stay away from the pier edge at night.** The water bubbles first.
- The previous keeper's notes and radio voices (Russian voice acting).
- Two difficulties: Normal and Nightmare. 12 achievements and a records log.
- The UI is the keeper's logbook: paper, ink and rubber stamps.

**Controls:** WASD move, E interact, F lantern, RMB/C focus beam, Shift run, Esc menu.

Platform: Windows (64-bit). Language: Russian (UI and voice).
